This is a third-version entry summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news in one 1024x1024 picture, rebuilt from scratch in a bold flat-colour design: 37 stories in total, told through 13 drawn cards — each with its own colour scene panel and a banner word baked into the art — led by COP31 ending with no deal in Antalya, Russian strikes killing 15 near Kyiv, 24 dead in the Banten factory fire, and the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide. Around the cards sit a colour-coded region bar counting all seven regions, a seven-number strip drawn from seven different stories high on the page, a "Day in UTC" timeline, a 24-line ledger of further stories beside a pinned world map, and a printed date check separating the day's news from older and not-yet stories; every card carries a verification tag and names its sources. The page is shipped as data in art.png and main.py copies it deterministically to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

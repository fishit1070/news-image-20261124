This entry sums up the whole of Tuesday 24 November 2026's world news in a single 1024x1024 picture, packing 37 stories into one page. The heart of the page is a set of 13 drawn cards, led by COP31 ending with no deal in Antalya, Russian strikes killing 15 near Kyiv, 24 dead in the Banten factory fire, and the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide. Around the cards sit a colour-coded region bar, a strip of seven numbers drawn from seven different stories, and a "Day in UTC" timeline tracing the day hour by hour. A 24-line ledger of further stories runs beside a pinned world map, and a printed date check separates the day's genuine news from older and not-yet-happened items. Every card carries a verification tag and names its sources, and the finished page ships as data in art.png, which main.py copies deterministically into the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
